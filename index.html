Customer support and complaints

SLOTORO confirms live chat, email support and a Data Protection Officer contact. Phone support is not confirmed, so complex payout or document issues should be written down clearly before you start a chat.

Live chat support

Live chat is confirmed 24/7. Support languages are English, German, Italian, Bulgarian, Greek, Hungarian, Polish, Portuguese and Romanian. Use chat for quick bonus, login and app questions.

Email and ticket system

Use support@slotoro.bet for documents, payout disputes and account verification trails. For privacy matters, use dpo@slotoro.bet.

How to file a complaint

Collect account email, transaction ID, bonus name, screenshots and chat transcript. Start with live chat, then email if the answer needs documents or escalation.

For KYC, do not send cropped or altered documents unless support asks for a masked card view. SLOTORO explicitly forbids third-party cards and anonymous payment tools.
