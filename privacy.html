Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SLOTORO handles personal data when a visitor opens the website, creates an account, uses the cashier, plays games, contacts support or takes part in promotions. The website is operated by Wiraon B.V.; Briantie Limited is involved in payment processing and co-data-controller functions.

1. Data Controller and Contacts

Wiraon B.V. is the main operator responsible for account and gaming data. Privacy requests can be sent to dpo@slotoro.bet. General account, payout or verification questions can be sent to support@slotoro.bet.

2. Personal Data SLOTORO May Collect

SLOTORO may collect account details such as name, date of birth, email, phone number, login credentials, account currency, language preference and communication history. Technical data can include IP address, device type, browser, operating system, session identifiers, cookies, pages visited and security logs.

For payments and verification, SLOTORO may process deposit and withdrawal records, payment method details, wallet identifiers, transaction IDs, KYC documents, a selfie with documents and payment-card images where required. If a card photo is requested, hide the CVV and middle six digits unless support gives a different lawful instruction.

3. Why the Data Is Used

Personal data is used to create and manage accounts, provide games and sportsbook access, process deposits and withdrawals, run bonuses, answer support requests, verify age and identity, detect fraud or bonus abuse, prevent payment misuse, manage self-exclusion and apply responsible-gambling limits.

SLOTORO may also use data for security monitoring, service analytics, troubleshooting, legal or regulatory recordkeeping, dispute handling and marketing where the user has not opted out or where consent is required and has been given.

4. Grounds for Processing

SLOTORO processes data when it is needed to provide the requested service, comply with legal and regulatory duties, protect legitimate business and security interests, investigate abuse, or follow the user’s consent for optional communications and non-essential cookies.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies help keep users signed in, protect sessions, remember preferences, measure website performance, detect suspicious activity and support marketing settings. Essential cookies cannot be switched off without breaking core features, while analytics and marketing cookies follow the choices made in the cookie tools or the browser.

The Cookie Policy describes cookie types, purposes and management options in more detail.

6. KYC, AML and Risk Checks

Online gambling services must check age, identity, payment ownership and suspicious activity. Document requests usually come at the withdrawal stage or after volume and risk triggers. Refusing verification, using third-party payment tools or sending inconsistent documents can delay or stop transactions.

7. Sharing Personal Data

SLOTORO may share personal data with payment processors, KYC and fraud-prevention providers, game providers, hosting and security vendors, customer-support tools, analytics services, professional advisers, group companies and authorities where disclosure is required or reasonably necessary.

Service providers receive only the data needed for their task and may not use it for unrelated purposes. SLOTORO does not treat account documents or payment verification files as ordinary marketing data.

8. International Processing

Because gambling, payment, hosting and support services can use partners in different jurisdictions, personal data may be processed outside the user’s place of residence. SLOTORO uses contractual, technical and organisational safeguards when transferring data to service providers.

9. Data Security

SLOTORO lists security measures such as SSL encryption, firewalls, RNG certification claims and Gamecheck SEAL verification. Users also carry a practical role in security: use a unique password, keep payment details consistent, do not share account access and contact support quickly if an account or payment method looks compromised.

10. Data Retention

SLOTORO keeps personal data only for as long as it is needed for account operation, legal and regulatory duties, payment records, anti-fraud work, responsible-gambling obligations, dispute handling and security logs. Some information, such as self-exclusion records and transaction history, may need to remain after account closure to prevent harm, abuse or unlawful re-registration.

11. User Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, users may request access to their data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction, objection to certain processing, a copy of portable data, withdrawal of consent and review of marketing preferences. SLOTORO may need to verify identity before answering a request and may keep data where retention is required for legal, payment, security or responsible-gambling reasons.

12. Marketing Communications

Users may receive casino news, bonuses and promotional messages if they choose to receive them or where the account settings allow such communication. Marketing messages include a way to unsubscribe or change preferences. Operational messages about security, KYC, payments, terms or account status may still be sent even after marketing is turned off.

13. Age Limit and Children’s Privacy

SLOTORO is for adults only. The site requires users to confirm that they are at least 18 years old. SLOTORO does not knowingly collect data from minors, and accounts that fail age checks can be restricted or closed.

14. Changes to This Policy

SLOTORO may update this Privacy Policy when services, data practices, vendors, security measures or legal requirements change. Users can review the policy before registration, large withdrawals or document submission.

15. Contact

For privacy requests, contact dpo@slotoro.bet. For support, verification, payments or account questions, contact support@slotoro.bet. Include the account email, request type and enough detail to identify the issue, but do not send unrequested card images, passwords or full payment credentials.
