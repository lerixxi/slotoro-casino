Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how SLOTORO uses cookies, local storage, pixels, tags and similar technologies when visitors browse the website, create an account, use the cashier, launch games, contact support or manage privacy choices. Cookie data is handled by the operator, Wiraon B.V., together with Briantie Limited where payment processing is involved.

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files placed on a browser or device. Similar technologies can store session IDs, remember interface settings, measure page performance, detect suspicious traffic or connect a visit with a login, game session, payment attempt or support request.

2. Why SLOTORO Uses Cookies

SLOTORO uses cookies to keep the website functional, protect accounts, maintain login sessions, remember age-gate and preference choices, support cashier routing, connect game-provider sessions, improve page speed, investigate errors, prevent abuse and manage marketing permissions.

Some cookies are necessary for the site to work. Optional analytics or marketing cookies depend on available consent tools, browser settings and the choices a user makes while using the website.

3. Strictly Necessary Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies support core functions: page loading, secure login, account session continuity, fraud controls, payment flow, language or currency display, responsible-gambling tools and basic security checks. Blocking these cookies can break login, cashier access, game launch or account-protection features.

4. Security and Verification Cookies

Online gambling accounts need careful security controls. Cookies and device signals can help SLOTORO detect unusual login patterns, repeated failed access, payment mismatch, bonus abuse, restricted-account activity, proxy use, bot traffic and other behaviour that may require KYC or AML review.

5. Preference and Performance Cookies

Preference cookies remember practical choices such as display settings, accepted notices, account-area state and interface behaviour. Performance cookies help SLOTORO understand whether pages, forms, payment screens and game tiles load cleanly. They turn scattered clicks into useful maintenance signals instead of guesswork.

6. Analytics and Marketing Cookies

Analytics cookies can measure visits, page paths, errors, device types and campaign performance. Marketing cookies can help manage bonus messaging, affiliate attribution, frequency limits and opt-out choices. Ordinary marketing measurement works without KYC documents or full payment details.

7. Third-Party Technologies

Game providers, payment services, KYC tools, fraud-prevention vendors, hosting providers, analytics tools, affiliate systems and customer-support platforms can set or read cookies when their services load on the website. Each partner processes that data under its own service terms and privacy commitments.

8. Managing Cookie Choices

Users can manage non-essential cookies through available website controls or browser settings. Browser tools can delete cookies, block third-party cookies or limit tracking signals. Strict blocking may make a gambling site feel broken fast: login, cashier checks, games, support chat and responsible-gambling tools can depend on stored session data.

9. Cookie Retention

Session cookies usually expire when the browser closes. Persistent cookies can remain longer to remember choices, protect the account, support fraud checks or meet legal and operational recordkeeping needs. Exact names, providers and retention periods can change when SLOTORO updates its website, payment routing, security tools or game integrations.

10. Privacy Requests and Updates

Questions about cookies, privacy choices or data requests can be sent to dpo@slotoro.bet. Account, bonus and payment matters belong with the support team at support@slotoro.bet. A revised Cookie Policy is published on this page whenever website functions, vendors or legal duties change.
